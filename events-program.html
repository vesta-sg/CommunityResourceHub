Community Events Program

Local festivals, theater, and community activities that bring residents together and strengthen connections.

About Community Events

Community events help create stronger neighborhoods by bringing people together through shared experiences. Festivals, theater performances, and workshops allow residents to meet others, support local organizations, and participate in meaningful activities. These events encourage creativity, volunteering, and community pride while giving families and students safe and engaging things to do.

Recommended Programs

One great local option is Act1 Theater, which offers affordable shows, volunteer opportunities, and productions such as Tuck Everlasting. It’s a great place for students and families to support the arts and get involved in the community. Another strong option is Merely Players, a theater group known for a wide variety of performances scheduled throughout the year. While Act1 Theater is typically more affordable and community-focused, Merely Players offers a broader range of productions and entertainment choices.

Why It Matters

Participating in community events builds connections, supports local artists, and gives residents opportunities to learn new skills and enjoy shared experiences. These programs strengthen community engagement and create welcoming spaces for people of all ages. By promoting events like theater and workshops, the Community Resource Hub helps residents stay involved and connected.
