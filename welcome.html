Chattahooche Resource Hub

The location chosen for this project is Caswyck Trace in Johns Creek. This area was selected because it represents a real residential community with nearby schools, parks, and local services. By focusing on a specific neighborhood, the resource hub becomes more authentic and useful. The goal is to provide residents with accurate and relevant information about nearby resources they can realistically access..

Food Assistance

Our food resources include local food banks and nonprofit programs that help families access nutritious meals. These services ensure no one in our community goes hungry.

Youth Programs

Youth programs provide mentorship, after-school activities, and leadership opportunities. These resources help children and teens grow, stay engaged, and develop essential life skills.

Tutoring & Academic Support

Tutoring centers and educational programs are included to help students succeed in school, improve grades, and prepare for exams.

Community Arts & Events

Local theaters, performances, and events bring residents together, encourage creativity, and strengthen the sense of community.

Volunteering Opportunities

Volunteering organizations are listed to help people give back, gain experience, and participate in community service activities.

Healthcare Services

Clinics and medical providers ensure residents have access to physical and mental healthcare, promoting overall community well-being.
