Welcome to the Chattahoochee Resource Hub

The location chosen for this project is Caswyck Trace in Johns Creek. This area was selected because it represents a real residential community with nearby schools, parks, and local services. By focusing on a specific neighborhood, the resource hub becomes more authentic and useful. The goal is to provide residents with accurate and relevant information about nearby resources they can realistically access.

Overview

Food Assistance
Our food resources include local food banks and nonprofit programs that help families access nutritious meals.
Youth Programs
Youth programs provide mentorship, after-school activities, and leadership opportunities.
Tutoring & Academic Support
Tutoring centers and educational programs are included to help students succeed.
Community Arts & Events
Local theaters, performances, and events bring residents together.
Volunteering Opportunities
Volunteering organizations help people give back and gain experience.
Healthcare Services
Clinics and medical providers ensure residents have access to healthcare.
